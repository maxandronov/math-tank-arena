How to Play

Objective

  • Destroy the enemy base (top-right) before the bot destroys yours (bottom-left). Base HP shows in your HUD.

Controls

  • WASD or Arrow keys move · Mouse aim · Space/Click shoot
  • R reload · Q shop · Esc pause
  • On a touch device, pick Touch (Joystick) below for an on-screen joystick, tap-to-shoot, and Reload/Shop/Pause buttons.

The Math Twist

  • Reloading and shop purchases both require answering a quick math question against a timer.
  • Fast, correct answers pay the most scraps; a second try pays less; a timeout costs you the reload/purchase and reveals the answer.

Blocks & Scraps

  • Wood, brick, stone and metal blocks are scattered on the field — tougher tiers take more hits (or a stronger cannon) but pay out more scraps when destroyed.

Supply Depot

  • Spend scraps (Q) on more ammo, armor, speed, or cannon power — or place your own defensive barrier blocks.

The Enemy Bot

  • It farms blocks, fights back if you get close, and will periodically push to attack your base — don't leave it undefended!
  • Destroyed tanks respawn back at their base after a few seconds.
